# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want this project to be a place where anyone can learn from the code and ask
questions without fear of being made to feel small. Everyone participating —
issues, pull requests, discussions — is expected to act accordingly.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and assume good faith. A question that seems basic to you may be
  someone's first week with agentic systems.
- Give and accept feedback on the work, not the person.
- Respect that maintainers' time is finite; a change may be declined without it
  being a judgement on you.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory or sexualised language, publishing
someone's private information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Report a problem to **conduct@knowlytix.ai**. Reports are handled privately.
Maintainers may edit, hide or delete contributions, and may block accounts, for
behaviour they judge to violate this document.
