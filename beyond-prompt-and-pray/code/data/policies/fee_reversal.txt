Fee Reversal Eligibility

A representative may approve a fee reversal of up to $35 per occurrence
under all of the following conditions: account in good standing for at
least 12 months; no prior reversal in the same calendar year; customer
relationship value above the standard threshold.

Reversals greater than $35 require manager approval. Agents must not
commit the bank to a fee waiver without authorization. Escalate the
request to a representative who can authorize the reversal.
