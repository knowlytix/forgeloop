Regulatory Escalation Requirements

Any complaint that alleges unfair, deceptive, or abusive acts or practices
(UDAAP) must be escalated to the Compliance team within one business
day. This includes but is not limited to:

- Allegations of discriminatory treatment
- Disputes citing improper fee assessment under Regulation E or Regulation Z
- Complaints involving mortgage servicing under Regulation X
- Anything involving customer harm exceeding five hundred dollars

See: 12 USC §5531 (CFPB UDAAP authority).
