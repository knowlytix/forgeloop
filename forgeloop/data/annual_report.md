## 6. Management Discussion and Analysis

Fiscal 2025 was a year of disciplined growth. Management attributes the increase
in consolidated revenue to sustained demand in the Technology division, where the
Cloud Platform segment continued to lead the company's topline, and to improved
operating leverage in Operations. The company expanded margins through cost
control in its supply chain and a deliberate shift toward higher-value service
contracts. Management does not consider any single customer to be material to
consolidated results, and believes the segment mix provides resilience against a
downturn in any one end market. These remarks are qualitative; the authoritative
figures are those reported in the tables above.

## 7. Risk Factors

The company is exposed to a range of risks that could cause actual results to
differ from expectations. Competition in cloud services is intense and could
pressure pricing. The Operations division depends on third-party logistics
providers, and disruption to those providers could raise costs or delay delivery.
Currency movements affect the Europe-based Operations division when results are
translated into US dollars. Regulatory change in data privacy and consumer
protection could increase compliance costs. None of these risk factors carries a
specific quantified estimate in this section.

## 8. Outlook

For fiscal 2026, management expects continued investment in the Cloud Platform
segment and a measured expansion of the Retail footprint. The company intends to
maintain its current dividend policy and to prioritize reinvestment over
acquisitions. Forward-looking statements in this section are subject to the risk
factors above and are not guarantees of future performance.
