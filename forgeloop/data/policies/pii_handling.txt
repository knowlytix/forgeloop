PII Handling Policy

Customer personally identifiable information (PII) must never be
transmitted over unencrypted channels. PII in customer communications
must be redacted before forwarding or quoting. Any inadvertent PII
exposure must be reported to the Privacy team within 24 hours.

PII includes: Social Security numbers, full account numbers, dates of
birth, mother's maiden name and any combination of name plus address
plus date of birth.
